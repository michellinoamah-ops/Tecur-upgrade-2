Questions to ask

What are you paying for?

Open the section that matches your visit.

01 I’m buying a spare part

“Will this fit my own car?” Give the model, year and chassis number (VIN) if needed. Ask whether the part is new or used, and whether the price includes fitting.

Before paying, ask what happens if the part supplied does not fit. Keep the receipt and any warranty details.

02 I’m taking my car to the workshop

“What does this amount cover?” Ask for the cost of checking the fault, the parts and the labour. If you only want the car checked, say so before work starts.

Ask the team to contact you with a new price if they find more work is needed.

03 I need a mechanic to come to me

“Is the call-out fee separate?” A call-out fee covers the trip to your location. Ask whether it includes checking the car and what you would pay if the repair needs workshop equipment.

Give your area and a nearby landmark so the team can confirm whether a visit is possible.
