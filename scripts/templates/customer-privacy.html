This notice explains how Tecur Automotive uses your information and what to expect when using our website or asking us for parts and vehicle services.

Your privacy

Information you choose to share

When you contact us, we may receive your name, phone number, vehicle details, service request, preferred date and the area you want us to visit. You can also choose to send photos of your vehicle or a part. Please leave out bank details, identity documents, faces and other information we do not need.

Photos and location are optional

You can make an enquiry without uploading photos or sharing your device’s location. You can remove selected photos or a location pin before sending. Photos help us understand your request; they do not replace an inspection or confirm that a part will fit.

For mobile service, you can choose to add your current position. If you also choose live sharing, your device may send updated positions for up to 30 minutes after you submit your enquiry directly to us. Keep the page open. Updates may pause if your phone locks, the page closes or your connection drops. Use “Stop live location” to stop further updates. The last position already sent remains part of your enquiry unless you ask us to remove it and we have no lawful reason to keep it.

Why we use your information

We use your details to answer your enquiry, discuss parts or repairs, prepare a quotation, arrange a visit and keep you informed about the work you request. We use optional photos and location with your permission. We also process information where needed to take steps you request before a service, carry out an agreed service, meet legal duties and protect the website from misuse. We do not sell your personal information or use enquiry photos in advertising without separate permission.

Who may receive it

Your details are available to the people handling your enquiry and, where necessary, the technician attending your vehicle. Service providers that help us operate the website, store information or communicate with you may process information for those purposes. Some providers may process data outside Nigeria; applicable safeguards must be used where required. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

If you choose WhatsApp, your message, any photos you attach in the chat and any location you share there are handled under WhatsApp’s privacy terms as well. A map link opens the map provider’s service. If you chose photos on our website, attach them again in the WhatsApp chat. Live sharing in WhatsApp is controlled separately within WhatsApp.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details, photos and location information only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide the requested service and resolve related questions. Records may need to be kept longer for legal, accounting or dispute purposes. You can contact us to ask for deletion; we will explain if we need to keep any information.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access your information, correct it, delete it, limit its use, object to certain uses or receive a portable copy where the law provides these rights. You may withdraw permission for optional uses at any time. This applies from the time you withdraw permission. We may ask you to confirm who you are before we act on your request.

Contact tecurautomotive@gmail.com or call +234 708 599 0547. If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Information saved on your device

The vehicle planner, saved parts and checklist can save your choices in your browser. Announcement preferences may also be saved so that a dismissed notice does not keep appearing. You can clear this information through your browser settings or the relevant clear button. These features may work differently if browser storage is blocked. Basic connection information may be used to deliver pages and help prevent abuse. No advertising trackers are installed.

Keeping information safe

We use reasonable measures to protect information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No online service can promise complete security. Avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information and contact us if you think your details have been misused.

Website and service terms

Enquiries and appointments

Sending an enquiry is not a confirmed booking, purchase or permission to begin repairs. We will discuss availability, the proposed work, any inspection or call-out charge and the next steps with you. Please give accurate contact and vehicle details, and tell us promptly if your plans change.

Parts, prices and compatibility

Catalogue entries and photographs help you explore our services and parts. Some images are illustrative. Confirm the exact item, condition, model compatibility, availability, price, delivery or installation charges and any warranty before paying. A model name or photo may not tell us which part fits. We may ask for your chassis number (VIN) or the number on the old part.

Mobile service

Mobile visits depend on your location, the work required, safe access and technician availability. A shared location does not guarantee an arrival time or that the work can be completed on-site. Some repairs require workshop equipment. Please provide a safe, accessible place for the technician to assess the vehicle.

Advice and safety

Website guides and planning tools provide general information. They are not a mechanical diagnosis or a guarantee that a vehicle is safe to drive. If your vehicle has a serious fault, stop using it when safe and seek professional help. Do not use the website while driving.

Photos and fair use

Only upload photos you own or have permission to share and that relate to your enquiry. Do not submit harmful files, unlawful material or other people’s private information. You allow us to use submitted photos to assess and handle your request; you keep your rights in them.

Payments, changes and complaints

Any payment, cancellation, return and warranty terms for a particular order or service should be agreed with you before you proceed. If you have a problem, contact us with your enquiry or receipt details so we can review it. Nothing on this page removes rights you have under applicable consumer law.
