ADD THE APP.

No app-store download needed.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Open the Share menu and choose Add to Home Screen. You may need to scroll through the actions.
  3. If shown, keep Open as Web App on. Tap Add.

On Android

Use the Install TECUR button when available. Or open your browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Installation options depend on your browser. You can always use the full website without installing.
